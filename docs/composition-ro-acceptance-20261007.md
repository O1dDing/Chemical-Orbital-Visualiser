Other atoms, including unrelated counterions, have a separate exclusive ledger bucket. Alkali, alkaline-earth and other metal composition is distinct from applicability of the valence-d pi analysis.
- Details show one complete, source-normalized NAO composition and actual radial-shell captions such as Cr 3d and Fe 4s. The hard 50% dominance sentence and duplicate minimal-reference percentages are removed. Original numbers, coefficients and occupations remain unchanged.
- Verified shared-spatial RO inputs can compare all three columns using the associated `(Falpha+Fbeta)/2` expectation. Details distinguish that expectation from the original RO effective energy. Switching back preserves source energies and puts incompatible side expectations outside the quantitative comparison. Current-view names, layout, copies and exports follow the selected definition. This does not diagonalize a new wavefunction or assert a unique RO effective Hamiltonian.
- Every required physical spin operator is independently qualified against the associated archive, basis, coefficient and density evidence. Missing, stale, non-Hermitian or incompatible data cannot fabricate common energies. Independent UHF/UKS spatial sets are not forced into the RO path.
- The ordinary pi summary comes from the shared spatial, complete-environment d response. Local spin/source records remain behind a separate expandable source section; they do not independently label the ligand. Actual occupied-metal backbond evidence is required before an acceptor-field response is called back-donation.

## Joined pi descriptor

In the complete orthonormal NAO environment, remove only the declared d–pi cross block, then restore it continuously. Rank-revealing column pivoting selects a complete independent d reference. Principal-angle tracking follows the whole spectral subspace; a fixed d reference holds the small Hermitian response. Higher/lower occupation ligand families are examined from the same reference to detect competing or inverted mechanisms. Large cancellation is not called negligible.

The starting rank check fixes a real defect exposed by square-pyramidal CuCl5: selecting the five individually largest d weights selected two partners of one direction and omitted another. A molecule-independent regression reproduces this failure. The real case now passes in 16 steps with minimum squared overlap 0.999233 and endpoint residual 4.04e-14 Ha; no tolerance was relaxed.

Projection conditioning is dimensionless and the occupied-d resolution is in electrons. Neither is compared directly with the Hartree error bound. An energy-rescaling regression verifies that both qualifications remain unchanged when the operator and its energy uncertainty are scaled together.

Group application uses correspondence with the tracked responsive spectral subspace, separately from atomic d population. A sigma-only direction, ligand partner or incidental high-virtual tail does not inherit a whole-sphere pi verdict. Default response resolution (1e-4 Ha) is a declared numerical display resolution, not a universal chemical weak/strong threshold.

This frozen one-electron response is **not** charge transfer, a self-consistent binding energy, NBO E(2), an excitation, an AOM parameter or aiLFT. Complete projector rotations test invariance within the declared reference; they do not remove dependence on the chemical definition of that reference.
