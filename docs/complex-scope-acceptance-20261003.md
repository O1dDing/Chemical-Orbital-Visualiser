# Complex orbital scope and brief-view acceptance — 2026-10-03

The previous brief view could retain high radial/background states and assign misleading pi relationships. A broad fragment space was treated as evidence that arbitrary pairs belonged together; all non-core metal shells could count as current valence; details displayed a different, incomplete reference projection. The change separates these decisions and uses complete, source-normalized composition throughout the final view.

## Resulting behavior

- Channels carry their centre radial shell, ligand-space type, spin and actual operator scope. The ordinary directional summary requires a supported current-valence d–pi relation. Auxiliary p, higher-radial and geometric transverse-p information retain their own research scope.
- A two-endpoint reduction must share a verified operator mode (or complete degenerate mode block) and an actual ordered donor/acceptor source edge. Two unrelated modes in one broad space cannot be paired. A multi-group mode retains its real members as one network, rather than expanding into every possible energy difference.
- Numerical support, direction evidence and eligibility for the main explanation are separate. An occupied–occupied relationship remains occupied-space mixing. A tiny tail or an auxiliary relation cannot protect an entire background group from brief filtering.
- Complete NAO composition uses the original member norm and averages over complete groups. Current centre s/p/d/f, other centre shells, ligand valence, other ligand space, core and unresolved remainder are exclusive categories. Ligand s/p subtotals are not counted twice. Details, copy, export and native inspection share the same ledger.
- Current radial shells are declared before filtering. Necessary ns/np can carry a source `Ryd` label; higher radial d/f do not become current valence merely by appearing in an already-retained MO. Occupied sigma-framework support is separately recovered from the complete first-shell donor NBO space, including ligand lone pairs and actual centre–ligand bond columns. The original overlap maps these into the canonical space; full source closure and orthogonality are checked.
- Verified shared-spatial RO alpha/beta donor projectors are averaged as spatial projectors; occupations remain unchanged. Explicit UHF spaces remain separate. Projector averaging does not inflate centre-p populations or double the reading budget.
- Filtering keeps complete central groups and their required side support. Unused corresponding AO/SALC groups and links fold together; shared necessary side objects remain. The full source remains recoverable. The local geometry label names the actual coordination skeleton, such as `CrN6`, separately from the full-molecule MO symmetry.
- Final displayed counts are calculated after filtering. A source MO identity is independent of its current-view occurrence number.

No molecule ID or molecular name selects a special-case result. Canonical coefficients, energies and occupations are unchanged. No new quantum-chemistry job was needed for this correction.
